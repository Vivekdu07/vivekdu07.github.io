About Me

I am Vivekanand Choubey, a PhD researcher in Chemistry at IIT Indore, working under the guidance of Prof. Selva Kumar Sermadurai. My current research focuses on the development of novel methodologies for the photocatalytic synthesis of organosilanes, with an emphasis on visible-light-driven catalytic transformations, sustainable C–Si bond formation, and innovative synthetic methodology development.

Prior to joining IIT Indore, I worked as a Project Associate-I at CSIR–Central Drug Research Institute (CSIR-CDRI), Lucknow, where I contributed to a DST-SERB sponsored project on transition metal-catalyzed enantioselective C–X bond formation, focusing on asymmetric synthesis and reaction optimization under the supervision of Dr. Nilanjana Majumdar, a former postdoctoral researcher from the group of Benjamin List (Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 2021).

Previously, I served as a Project Assistant at Shiv Nadar University, contributing to a DST-INSPIRE funded interdisciplinary research project focused on the biophysical characterization of extracellular vesicles using single-molecule detection methods, which strengthened my analytical and interdisciplinary research skills.

I hold an M.Sc. in Chemistry from the University of Delhi and have qualified several national-level competitive examinations, including CSIR-NET (AIR 54), GATE (AIR 1204), IIT-JAM (AIR 801), and the University of Delhi M.Sc. Entrance Examination (AIR 39).

My research interests lie in photoredox catalysis, synthetic organic chemistry, and catalytic methodology development, with the goal of designing efficient, sustainable, and innovative solutions for modern chemical synthesis.
